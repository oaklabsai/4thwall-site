01 —What's actually included?

No picking required. Every contractor gets the same six systems: lead response, booking, review generation, AI visibility, storm mode, and seasonal campaigns. What changes is how each one is configured — to your trade, your market, how you talk, what you work on.

02 —Is this another software tool I have to learn?

No. We build it. We run it. We tune it. You text us when you want something changed. The only thing you'll see is the dashboard — and that's optional.

03 —Will the AI sound like a robot?

No. It's trained on how you and your team actually talk — tone, vocabulary, what you say "no" to. We test every conversation flow before going live. Homeowners regularly don't realize they're talking to AI until you tell them.

04 —What if a lead asks something the AI can't handle?

It hands off to you with full context — who they are, what they need, what's been said. You walk into the conversation already informed.

05 —Do I have to switch off my CRM?

No. We sit on top of whatever you already use — field management, scheduling, CRM, all of it. If you change tools later, we adjust. The AI infrastructure is yours, not a platform's.

06 —What does it cost?

Less than a part-time office manager. Pricing depends on volume, languages, and scope — most contractors recover it inside the first month from leads that would have gone to voicemail.

07 —How long does deployment take?

48 hours from signed contract to live system. We've done it faster — two days is what we promise.

08 —What if I want to stop?

30-day notice, no penalty. We hand you the system or wind it down — your call. We've never had a contractor leave, but the door's not locked.
