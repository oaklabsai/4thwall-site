The contractor journey

Connect once. Control the result.

The useful part is not the integration itself. It is the private path from source data to an explanation you can inspect.

1

Connect

Authorize a read-only connection to a supported operating system. Lens records purpose, scope and source.

2

Translate

We turn raw records into a handful of plain, homeowner-ready facts—dropping anything private, personal or unsupported.

3

Review

Keep a record in the preview, keep it private, or question the source. Your decisions leave an audit trail.

4

Explain

Vesta answers the homeowner’s risk questions in plain language—with the source and limitation attached.

Your operating system stays the source. Lens is designed to keep the minimum translated evidence needed for the stated trust purpose—not a shadow copy of your CRM. Disconnect or erase at any time: erasure removes the translated evidence, and only the audit trail of your consent and decisions remains as an integrity record.
