FAQ · 05

The honest
questions.

01 —Is this a package?

No. There are no tiers, no bundles, no fixed feature sets. We scope each engagement around your actual operation — what's breaking, what's manual, what's costing you the most. Scope is determined by what your business actually needs, not by a pricing page.

02 —How is this different from an ops manager or consultant?

A consultant tells you what to build, then leaves. An ops manager needs managing themselves. We build the infrastructure, deploy it, and run it permanently — no ramp-up, no management overhead.

03 —Do you need access to our tools and data?

We configure connections together in a single session. You grant access, we set up integrations, and from that point the system runs on your infrastructure. We don't maintain ongoing credential access — everything lives under your control.

04 —How long before something is actually running?

Most clients have their first live system within the first week. We read what you share, build fast, and add from there.

05 —What determines the scope and the price?

Scope is determined by what's actually broken or manual in your operation. We talk through it before any number is mentioned — no tiers, no guesswork. Price follows scope, not the other way around.

06 —What does ongoing management actually look like?

One contact, everything handled. We monitor, tune, and expand the system as the business changes. The system is never "finished" — it keeps improving as your operation does. You don't manage us; we manage the infrastructure.
