for contractors · atlas

Not a receptionist.
The whole front office.

Atlas runs every part of the business that happens around the job — the answering, the qualifying, the booking, the follow-up, the reviews, the campaigns — as one managed service, in your name.

Most contractors don’t lose work because they’re bad at the job. They lose it in the gaps.

The call that came in while you were on a roof. The estimate that never got a second text. The happy customer nobody asked for a review. The client who’d forgotten your name by spring. Each one is quiet — and each one is a job that should have been yours.

Atlas is the managed front office that closes those gaps. It answers and qualifies the lead, books the estimate with the context attached, follows up on the quote, asks for the review the moment the job lands, and runs the seasonal and storm campaigns that pull past customers back — all measured, all in your name.

Every month ends with a receipt: what came in, what got answered, what got booked. Not a dashboard you have to run. A record that compounds — into reviews, into reputation, into the next job finding you first.
